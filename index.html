Minnesota Speed Limits

  • Alley: 10 mph
  • Residential (if adopted): 25 mph
  • Urban district: 30 mph
  • Interstate (urban 50,000+): 65 mph
  • Non-interstate freeway: 65 mph
  • Interstate (rural): 70 mph
  • 100+ mph = Automatic 6-month license revocation

DUI/DWI Laws

  • BAC Limit (21+): 0.08%
  • BAC Limit (Commercial): 0.04%
  • Implied Consent: Refusing a chemical test is a criminal offense
  • Open Container: No open alcohol in vehicle - ever
  • Body processes: ~1 drink per hour

School Bus Rules

  • Undivided road: ALL traffic from BOTH directions must stop
  • Divided highway (median/barrier): Opposite direction may proceed
  • Stop distance: At least 20 feet from the bus

Road Signs by Shape & Color

  • Octagon (8 sides): Stop
  • Triangle (inverted): Yield
  • Pentagon (5 sides): School zone
  • Pennant: No passing zone
  • Round: Railroad crossing ahead
  • Diamond: Warning
  • Orange: Construction/work zone
  • Brown: Recreation/public areas
  • Green: Guide/direction signs

Important Minnesota Rules

  • Move Over Law: Slow down and move over for stopped emergency vehicles
  • Zipper Merge: Stay in lane until merge point, then take turns
  • Headlights required: Sunset to sunrise and any time visibility is impaired
  • Following distance: Minimum 3 seconds (more in bad conditions)
  • Ice stopping distance: Up to 10x longer than dry pavement
  • Liability insurance: Required for all vehicles
  • Cyclists: Same rights as motor vehicles; give 3 feet clearance
  • Motorcycles: Full lane use; illegal to share a lane with them
  • Stranded in snow: Stay in the car
  • Skid on ice: Ease off gas, steer in direction you want to go

Traffic Signals

  • Flashing yellow: Slow down, proceed with caution
  • Flashing red: Stop (treat as stop sign)
  • Solid yellow arrow: Protected turn is ending
  • Red arrow: Stop; no turn in that direction
  • Green arrow to solid green: May still turn but must yield
  • Power outage: Treat as four-way stop
